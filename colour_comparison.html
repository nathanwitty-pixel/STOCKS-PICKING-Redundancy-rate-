Marketing Bags For Shoot (Stock rate — units sold ÷ stock at the start)

For the period at the top of the page: the stock in the shops on its first day (before that day's sales) and the units sold up to its last day. Sold rate = sold ÷ stock at the start. Above 100 % means stock received during the period was sold too. Stock rate = stock now ÷ stock at the start. Stock now is live shop stock. Signal: Push #1–3 = each bag's 3 colours with the most stock now. Offer comes from the Marketing dashboard's offer list for the month the period ends in: Kenya = Power Deal, Deal of the Week, Combo (bags_offer_source.json); Sinza / Uganda = Combo and Single offers (offers_monthly.csv); plus this month's new products.

Colour mix — stock vs sales

Each colour's share of the market's shop stock beside its share of units sold (colours as named in Odoo). A colour with a bigger sales share than stock share is selling faster than it is stocked (restock it); the opposite is redundant stock sitting on the shelf (push it). Click a column name to sort; the boxes under the names filter the table.

Colour comparison by bag

Category → bag → colour, like the marketing colour comparison sheet. Redundancy % = (stock − sold) ÷ stock: the share of today's stock the period's sales didn't cover (100 % = nothing sold; below 0 = it sold more than it has). A bag's row shows the average of its colours' rates, like the sheet's total row. Sales mix / stock mix = the colour's share of its bag's sales and stock. Offer = the bag's offers that month (Marketing dashboard). Click a bag to fold its colours.
